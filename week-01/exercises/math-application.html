In plain English

One project, math-application, one class, MathApp, with a main method that answers eight math questions and prints each answer.

Readability counts: a comment and a blank line between questions, meaningful names (never num1), and messages that tell the reader what the number means.

Each question points at a method of the Math class. Look it up rather than doing the math by hand.

The one idea

The Math class does the arithmetic you would otherwise write by hand. Your job is the rest: good names, clear messages, and a program a stranger can read.
