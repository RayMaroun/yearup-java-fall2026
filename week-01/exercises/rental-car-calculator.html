Rental Car Company

A program that asks six questions about a car rental and prints four amounts: rental, options, surcharge, total.

What you are building

The biggest program of the week: it asks six questions about a rental and prints four amounts.

Nothing in it is new. It is everything from the week together: Scanner, if, boolean, arithmetic, printf. The hard part is the order and the plan, which is why the workbook makes you do it on paper first.

Enter pickup date (mm/dd/yyyy): 10/20/2026 Enter number of rental days: 3 Do you want an electronic toll tag (y/n)? y Do you want a GPS (y/n)? n Do you want roadside assistance (y/n)? y Enter your age: 22 Rental Summary Basic car rental: $89.97 Options cost: $23.70 Underage driver surcharge: $26.99 Total cost: $140.66 Process finished with exit code 0

The wording is yours and will differ; the four amounts are what to check.

Where people get stuck

  • The yes/no question after the days gets skipped. The leftover Enter again, after nextInt().
  • Surcharge on the wrong amount. It is 30 percent of the basic rental only, not of the total.
  • Options charged once. They are per day: multiply by the days.
  • 140.66000000000003. The raw double. Format it to two decimals.

The one idea

Plan on paper, work two scenarios by hand, then make the program agree with your paper. When the totals match, you are done; when they do not, the paper tells you which amount to look at.
