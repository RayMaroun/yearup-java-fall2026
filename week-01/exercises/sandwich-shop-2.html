Sandwich Shop, Loaded

Your finished sandwich shop, with one more question: would you like it loaded?

What you are building

The same program as the last exercise, with one more question between the size and the age: loaded? A yes adds $1.00 to a Regular or $1.75 to a Large, before the discount.

The lesson is adding a feature to working code without breaking what already works, plus the two traps that come with it: reading a word right after a number, and comparing words.

What a finished run looks like

run
Enter sandwich size (1 for Regular, 2 for Large): 1 Would you like it loaded? (yes/no): yes Enter age: 16 Cost of sandwich: $5.81 Enter sandwich size (1 for Regular, 2 for Large): 2 Would you like it loaded? (yes/no): no Enter age: 70 Cost of sandwich: $7.16

Your prompts may be worded differently. The amounts are what to check.

Where people get stuck

  • The loaded question is skipped. That is the leftover Enter after the size. One extra nextLine() before the real one eats it.
  • Yes never matches. Comparing the answer with == never matches typed text. Use equalsIgnoreCase().
  • Total is off. The loaded cost was added after the discount. It is part of the price that gets discounted, so add it before.
  • "No" or "YES" breaks it. A capital letter must still work, and ignoring case is what gives you that.

The one idea

Change working code one question at a time. Commit before and after, so the version that worked is always one click away, and compare words with equals, never ==.
