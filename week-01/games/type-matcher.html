Type Matcher

A literal flashes up. Click the type that can hold it, or press its number key. The quotes and the suffix letters are the clues.

Press Start. Keys 1 to 7 pick a type.

single quotes = char · double quotes = String · L = long · f = float · a decimal point with no letter = double

The one idea

The literal tells you its type before the variable does. Quotes make text or a char, a decimal point makes a double, and the letters L and f change the size. Read the value, and the type follows.
