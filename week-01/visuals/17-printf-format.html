The one idea

Placeholders are filled left to right from the arguments: the 1st % takes the 1st argument, the 2nd takes the 2nd. The letter after % says how to format the value: s text, d whole number, f decimal. A number before it sets the width (a minus means left aligned), .2 sets two decimals, a comma groups digits, and %n ends the line.
