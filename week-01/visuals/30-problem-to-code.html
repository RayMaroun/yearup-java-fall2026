The one idea

Before you type, underline the nouns and the verbs. The nouns are your data: inputs become Scanner reads, outputs become printf, fixed values become numbers in the code. The verbs are your steps, in order; a step that depends on an answer is an if/else, and a calculation with a name is a method. The code follows the plan.
