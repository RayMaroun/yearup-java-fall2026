The one idea

The repository is the project folder, never the folders above it. pluralsight, java-development and workbook-1 only hold projects. Mac: ~/Desktop/pluralsight/java-development/workbook-1. Windows: C:\Users\[username]\Desktop\pluralsight\java-development\workbook-1.
