The one idea

Java widens for free; narrowing needs a cast and you own whatever is lost. Along byte, short, int, long, float, double, moving right is automatic. Moving left does not compile until you write the cast, and then Java drops digits (123.4567890123 becomes 123.45679), cuts decimals ((int) 9.99 is 9) or wraps around ((byte) 1000 is -24), with no error and no warning.
