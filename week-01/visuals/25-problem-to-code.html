From Problem to Code

A problem statement is a program written in English. Mark the data and the steps first; then every line of the plan becomes a few lines of Java. Step through one small problem from first read to first run.

The problem

A print shop charges $0.10 per page for black and white and $0.25 per page for color. Ask the customer whether they want color and how many pages they need. Print the total cost, with two decimals.

The one idea

Before you type, underline the nouns and the verbs. The nouns are your data: inputs become Scanner reads, outputs become printf, fixed values become numbers in the code. The verbs are your steps, in order; a step that depends on an answer is an if/else, and a calculation with a name is a method. The code follows the plan.
