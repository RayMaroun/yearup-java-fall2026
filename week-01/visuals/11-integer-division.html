Integer Division and Modulo

Ask Java for 10 / 3 and it answers 3, not 3.333. Nothing is rounded. When both sides are int, the leftover is cut off, and % is the operator that hands that leftover back. Step through the division and watch where the missing piece goes.

The one idea

int divided by int gives an int. Java keeps the whole groups and throws the leftover away. It cuts, it never rounds, so 10 / 3 is 3 and 1 / 2 is 0. % gives you the leftover that / dropped: 10 % 3 is 1. Make one side a double and the leftover is shared out as a decimal instead.
