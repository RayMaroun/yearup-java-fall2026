The one idea

int divided by int gives an int. Java keeps the whole groups and throws the leftover away. It cuts, it never rounds, so 10 / 3 is 3 and 1 / 2 is 0. % gives you the leftover that / dropped: 10 % 3 is 1. Make both sides double and the leftover counts as a fraction of a group instead.
