The one idea

+ becomes concatenation the moment a String is on either side, and Java works left to right, one + at a time. So "Sum: " + 1 + 2 prints Sum: 12, while 1 + 2 + " apples" prints 3 apples. Put parentheses around the math you want done first: "Sum: " + (1 + 2) prints Sum: 3.
