Scanner Buffer

Everything you type, ENTER included, lands in a buffer that Scanner reads from. nextInt() takes the digits and leaves the line separator behind, so the next nextLine() returns an empty String without waiting. Step through it, then turn on the fix.

The one idea

nextInt() stops before the line separator and leaves it in the buffer. Another nextInt() skips it, but nextLine() reads up to it, finds nothing, and returns "". When you read a number and then a line, consume the leftover first with an extra myScanner.nextLine().
