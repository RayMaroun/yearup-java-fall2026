Source, Transformation, Destination

Three words to remember. Send the fruit through one at a time.

Source

data at rest

Data transformation

data in transit

keep only the red ones

Destination

data at rest

The one idea

Data starts at rest in a source, is in transit while it is transformed, and comes to rest again in a destination. The source is left as it was. Remember these three words: they come back when streams are introduced.
