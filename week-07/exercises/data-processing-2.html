Where people get stuck

  • Person is red in the loops Program. It moved, so the loops Program needs an import of com.pluralsight.Person.
  • Nothing prints after filter. A stream does nothing until its last step. End it with a method that collects or prints.
  • A stream used twice. A stream can be used once. Start a new one from the list each time.
  • The result of a chain is the wrong type. Look at what each method hands to the next: a stream of people, then of ages, then one value.
