HashMap Operations

Put, replace, remove, then walk the map two ways. Use the buttons on the map.

the map · a key tag on top, its value under it

HashMap<Integer, Product> idsAndProducts

The map is empty.

Drawn in the order Java walks the map, which is not the order you added.

Two pairs: key 1 holds the Potato card, key 2 the Tomato card. A tag is a key, a card is its value.

The one idea

put adds a pair, or replaces the value when the key is already there, because keys are unique. remove takes a pair out by its key. values() and keySet() are the two ways to walk the whole map.
