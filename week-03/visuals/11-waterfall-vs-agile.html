The one idea

Agile delivers something working every few weeks and welcomes change. Each short sprint plans, builds, tests and shows one feature, so a new requirement just moves the plan. Waterfall builds everything in one long pass and finds out at the end, when changes are expensive and the customer sees the app for the first time.
