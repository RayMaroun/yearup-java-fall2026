Three objects, all empty: one for a date, one for a time, one for both. Press now() on a card.

The one idea

Pick the class by what you need to store. A birthday is a LocalDate, a closing time a LocalTime, a timestamp a LocalDateTime. now() reads the computer's clock once and the object keeps that value. None of them has a time zone.
