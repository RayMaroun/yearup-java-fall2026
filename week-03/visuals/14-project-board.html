A new board has three columns. Every story starts in Todo, the backlog.

The one idea

The board shows who is on what, what is left and what is done. Each story is a card that starts in Todo, MVP cards on top, and carries its own acceptance criteria. It walks left to right, In Progress, QA/Testing, Done, and the code is committed when it reaches Done.
