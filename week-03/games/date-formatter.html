Date Formatter

The moment on the left never changes. A pattern appears: type exactly what format prints and press Enter. Every third round the output appears instead: pick the pattern that printed it (keys 1 to 4). Capitals count.

MM month · mm minutes · HH 0 to 23 · hh 1 to 12 · one letter = no padding · MMM / E short name, MMMM / EEEE full name · 'text' printed as is

The one idea

Every letter in a pattern is a field, and the count of letters is the style. Capital M is the month, small m the minutes; HH runs 0 to 23, hh needs an a beside it; three or four letters turn a number into a name. Anything in single quotes is printed as is.
