Online Store

A store front in the terminal: products from a file, a search, a cart with a total.

What you are building

Your own project in your own repository, like the first two workshops. The program loads products.csv into a list of Product objects. From the home screen the customer browses or searches the products, adds some to a cart, then views the cart with its total and checks out with a cash payment.

No logins, no real card payment, and the cart is gone when the program ends. Check Out takes cash and gives change.

Where people get stuck

  • NumberFormatException: For input string: "Price". The header line was read as a product. Read it once before the loop and ignore it.
  • Where does the cart live? It is a second ArrayList of Product. Adding puts the same Product from the inventory into it; nothing new to build.
  • The change is wrong by a cent. Doubles add up with tiny errors. Round the total to two decimals before you compare it with the cash.

The one idea

Two lists of the same Product. The inventory holds everything the file gave you; the cart holds what the customer picked. Every screen reads one of them or moves a product into the cart; check out empties it.
