Search Inventory

A store's products in an ArrayList, printed one line each, then three bonuses that grow it.

What you are building

The StoreApp from the workbook pages. A Product class holds one item (id, name, price), getInventory() returns an ArrayList of at least 5 products, and main prints them all.

No arrays with a fixed size and no counter: the list grows as you add. The file, the sort and the menu are bonuses, one at a time, after the basic version runs.

Where people get stuck

  • ArrayList is red. It needs import java.util.ArrayList; at the top.
  • Every character becomes a part. The pipe is special in split: split("\\|").
  • The price will not go in. Parts are Strings: Double.parseDouble for the price, Integer.parseInt for the id.
  • The menu skips a question. The Enter left after nextInt(): read the rest of the line.
