    The three relationships you know

    Every line in your diagram is one of these three.

    Has-a

    A backpack has several devices. The diamond sits on the one that holds; the star means several.

    In your shopWhat holds several of something? What can a customer add any number of?

    Is-a · inheritance

    A dog is an animal. A solid line, and the triangle touches the parent.

    In your shopAre some of your things a kind of the same thing? Say "is a" out loud: does it sound true?

    Interface

    A phone and a speaker can both play. A dashed line, and the triangle touches the interface.

    In your shopWhich of your things are different, but must answer the same question?
