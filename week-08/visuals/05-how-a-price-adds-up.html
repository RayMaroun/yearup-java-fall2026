How a Price Adds Up

One item, one receipt. Pick a size, press toppings, and watch each price land.

the receipt for this one item · press a line to take it off

Total

The one idea

An item starts at the price of its size, whatever type is chosen. Each premium topping adds its own price for that size, and an extra portion adds the extra price on top of it. Regular toppings, sauces and the special option are included: they add 0.00. Change the size, and the item and every premium topping on it change price together.
