Building One Item

The cashier asks seven questions, one at a time. Answer them and watch the item grow.

    the order

    The one idea

    One item is built from seven questions, always in the same order: the type, the size, the two kinds of premium toppings (each followed by "Extra?"), the regular toppings, the sauces, then the special option. A question that allows any number also allows none. When the last answer is in, the item goes onto the order and the application goes back to the order screen.
