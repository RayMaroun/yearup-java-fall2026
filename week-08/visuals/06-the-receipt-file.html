The one idea

One confirmed order, one receipt file, named by the moment of the confirmation. yyyyMMdd-HHmmss.txt reads year, month, day, a dash, hour, minute, second. Every piece keeps its number of digits (March is 03), and HH is the 24-hour clock, so 2 PM is 14. Each receipt goes into the receipts folder inside your project folder and holds the order details shown at checkout. 0) Cancel creates no file.
