Your Demo

Ten minutes, nine parts, in this order. Press a part and ask yourself its questions about your own project. Then press Rehearse and talk it through against the clock.

10:00 Ten minutes in all. Press Rehearse, start talking, and follow the part that lights up.

The one idea

Show your work in the order you made it. What it is, how you worked (commits, board, stories), what you designed (README and class diagram), what you wrote (the code), and then it running. Explain your own decisions: not too deep, not too vague.
