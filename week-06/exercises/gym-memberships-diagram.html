How this page works

Step 1 of the homework asks for the class diagram of the finished program, the way it looks after Step 12: Membership, BasicMembership, PremiumMembership and FamilyMembership.

In each step below you answer questions about the gym, with the hand-out in front of you, and the page draws what your answers say. Then a box marked Your turn tells you what to draw on your own in diagrams.net. The page starts the diagram. The rest is yours.

Mark what is abstract

In a class diagram, abstract is written in italics: the name of an abstract class, and the line of an abstract method. The hand-out makes two things abstract near the end, in Steps 11 and 12. Your diagram shows the program after Step 12, so it shows them.

Then export

  1. Keep the diagram file (File, Save). Step 13 of the hand-out asks you to fix the diagram and export it again.
  2. In IntelliJ, right-click the project root, New, Directory, and name it images. It sits at the same level as src and pom.xml.
  3. In diagrams.net: File, Export as, PNG, Export. Name it class-diagram.png and save it in that images folder.
  4. Commit and push, with a message in your own words.
  5. On GitHub, open images, then class-diagram.png. You should see your picture.
