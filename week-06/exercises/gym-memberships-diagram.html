Drawing the Class Diagram

Six steps from the hand-out to images/class-diagram.png. You practice here on library items, then you draw the gym on your own.

What Step 1 asks

A class diagram of the finished program, the way it looks after Step 12. Four classes: Membership, BasicMembership, PremiumMembership and FamilyMembership. Their fields with types and access, their methods, the arrows between them, and what is abstract. Exported as class-diagram.png into a folder images at the root of the project.

This page never shows the gym. Every example is a library: LibraryItem and its children Book, Dvd and Magazine. Same moves, different family.

0 of 6 done
1

Read the whole hand-out once, with the classes in mind

  • Step 1 comes first, but what each class knows and does is spread over Steps 2 to 12. Read them all before you draw.
  • Keep a list per class with two columns: what it knows and what it does.
  • Look for the lines that start with "Create", "One field of its own", "add" and "overrides". Each one adds to your list.
  • Read Steps 11 and 12 twice. They change the program near the end, and your diagram shows that end.

Done when: you have a list for each of the four classes.

3

Fill one box

Three bands: the name, the fields, the methods. Fields with a type and an access sign. Real methods, and constructors that take values. No plain getters and setters.

Press any line of the box

The box for the parent of the library family. Press a line to see what it says.
4

Parent and children, and the arrow

What every item has goes once, in the parent. A child box lists only its own field and the methods it writes again. Then one arrow from each child to the parent.

Which end gets the hollow triangle?

Constructors left out to keep the picture small.

A Book and a LibraryItem, one line between them. Pick the end that gets the triangle.
5

Mark what is abstract

The course writes abstract in italics: the class name, and each abstract method line. In diagrams.net, select the text and press Cmd+I (Mac) or Ctrl+I (Windows). Some things in the hand-out become abstract only in Steps 11 and 12. Your diagram shows the end, so draw them the way they are after Step 12.

Answer the question, the box answers back

Two questions, one at a time.
6

Draw yours in diagrams.net and export

  1. The project gym-memberships exists and is shared on GitHub (Step 1 of the hand-out).
  2. At app.diagrams.net draw your four boxes and the arrows. The reference shows the clicks for a box and a hollow triangle.
  3. Keep the diagram file too (File, Save). Step 13 asks you to fix it and export again.
  4. In IntelliJ, right-click the project root, New, Directory, name it images. Same level as src and pom.xml.
  5. In diagrams.net: File, Export as, PNG, Export. Name it class-diagram.png and save it in that images folder.
  6. In IntelliJ's commit panel tick the new file, write a message in your own words, Commit and Push.
  7. On GitHub open images, then class-diagram.png. You should see your picture.
