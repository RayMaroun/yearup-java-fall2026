Vehicle Inheritance

The classes from your Diagrams exercise as real Java: a Vehicle parent, four children, and a main that uses them.

What you are building

One project, vehicle-inheritance, with a parent class Vehicle and four child classes: Moped, Car, SemiTruck and Hovercraft. Vehicle holds four private properties and a getter and setter for each. Each child extends Vehicle. Main creates one of each and sets values with the setters.

No constructors in any class: they come in the next exercise. The structure is the one you designed in Diagrams, so your children may have extra properties of their own.

Yours will differ: your values, your wording. Check that every line comes from a getter, including the ones written in Vehicle.

Where people get stuck

  • The child has no getColor(). It does not need one. It inherits it from Vehicle. If the child does not say extends Vehicle, it has nothing.
  • Properties copied into the children. The four properties go only in Vehicle. Children keep only what you designed as their own.
  • A constructor appears. Do not write any constructor this time. If IntelliJ generated one, delete it.
  • "has private access". Main reaches a field directly. Go through its getter or setter.
