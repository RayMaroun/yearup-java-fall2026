Three arguments go into new Dog(...), but Dog has only one field of its own. The other two belong to Animal.

The one idea

The arguments named in super(...) go up to the parent's constructor; the child stores the rest. Dog cannot store name and age itself, they are private in Animal, so it hands them up. And super(...) is always the first statement: the parent part is built before the child does anything of its own.
