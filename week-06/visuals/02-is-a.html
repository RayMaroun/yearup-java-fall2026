Is-A

Say the sentence out loud. Pick a pair, then flip it.

The one idea

Inheritance is an is-a relationship, and it only reads one way. If "a Dog is an Animal" sounds true, Dog can extend Animal, and the arrow points from the child to the parent. If the sentence only works with "has a", that is an attribute, not inheritance.
