Reading a Class Diagram

Two classes, drawn the way developers sketch them. Tap any part.

This is a class diagram: one box per class, three bands in each box, and one arrow between them. Tap a band, a sign, a name, a type, or the arrow.

The one idea

A class box has three bands: the name, the attributes, the methods. The sign in front says who can reach a member: minus private, plus public, hash protected. The type comes after the colon. The hollow triangle always points at the parent. You will draw these same boxes yourself in diagrams.net.
