The one idea

equals asks "same text?" by checking the characters in pairs; equalsIgnoreCase does the same with M and m counted as one letter. compareTo asks "which comes first?": it stops at the first different pair and returns the gap between their codes. Negative means before, zero means same place, positive means after.
