The one idea

Make the fields private and give the outside world methods. A setter is the only way in, so it can check every value. A getter hands out a copy. Leave out the setter and the field is read-only. With no keyword at all, a member is package-private: visible inside its package, hidden from every other. Be explicit: write private or public.
