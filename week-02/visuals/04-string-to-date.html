The one idea

A String is not a date. Only yyyy-MM-dd text parses with no formatter, so "17/October/2002" needs a DateTimeFormatter whose pattern matches it exactly: d the day, MMMM the full month name, yyyy the year, every / in its place. Once parsed, println shows the date as yyyy-MM-dd.
