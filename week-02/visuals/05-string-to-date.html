The one idea

The pattern must describe the text exactly. MM reads a two digit month, d a day of one or two digits, MMM a short month name, yyyy a four digit year, and every / or space must match too. Without a formatter, LocalDate.parse expects yyyy-MM-dd. A LocalDate holds only three numbers: println shows them in ISO order, format shows them in any pattern you choose.
