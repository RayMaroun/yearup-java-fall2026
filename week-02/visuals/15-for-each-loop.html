for-each vs for

Two ways to visit every box. One knows where it is, one only knows what it holds.

The one idea

Both loops visit every box, in order. The indexed for knows the position, so you can use i to print it, skip, or change a box with nameList[i]. The for-each only gets a copy of each value: shorter to write, nothing to get wrong, but no position.
