The one idea

Scanner gives you a String of characters. Read the whole line with nextLine(), trim() it, then convert: Integer.parseInt for whole numbers, Double.parseDouble for decimals. Every character must belong to the number, or Java throws a NumberFormatException. When the line holds more than a number, split it first and parse one piece.
