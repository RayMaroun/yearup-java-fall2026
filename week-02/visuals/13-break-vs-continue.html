The one idea

break leaves the loop now: only 1 and 2 are added, 4 to 10 never run, the sum is 3. continue skips the rest of this pass: 3, 6 and 9 are not added, the loop keeps going, the sum is 37. The same 37 also comes without continue, by writing the if the other way: if (i % 3 != 0) { sum += i; }
