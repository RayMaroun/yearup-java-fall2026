Cell Phone Service

Your first class of your own: a CellPhone blueprint, one phone built from it, filled in from the keyboard.

What you are building

A program with two classes. CellPhone is the blueprint: five private fields, a no-argument constructor, a getter and a setter for each field. CellPhoneApplication holds main: it creates one phone, asks five questions, stores the answers through the setters and prints them back through the getters.

It does not dial anything and has only one phone; exercises 2 and 3 add that. The lesson is the split: the class describes a phone, main uses one.

Where people get stuck

  • The model question is skipped. That is the leftover Enter after the serial number. One extra nextLine() before the next question eats it.
  • "has private access" in red. main reached for a field directly. Go through the setter or the getter.
  • Everything prints 0 or blank. The setters were never called, or a setter's parameter has the field's name and this. is missing.
  • "cannot find symbol CellPhone". Both classes must sit in com.pluralsight, spelled the same way.

The one idea

A class is the blueprint, an object is one phone built from it. The fields stay private; the rest of the program goes through the getters and setters.
