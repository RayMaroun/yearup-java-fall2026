Vehicle Inventory

A used car lot in an array: six vehicles to start, a menu in a loop, and one method per option.

What you are building

A menu program for a used car dealership. A Vehicle class holds one car; an array of 20 holds the lot, with a counter of how many are in it. The menu lets the user list, search and add vehicles until they choose Quit.

No files and no database: the six vehicles are written into the code, and added vehicles are gone when the program ends. Start with options 1, 2 and 5; 3 and 4 come after.

Where people get stuck

  • NullPointerException on list. The loop went past the counter into empty slots.
  • The method cannot see the array. It lives in main. Pass it in, or make it a static field.
  • Add skips the make/model question. The leftover Enter after nextInt().
  • 6 does not quit. A break in a switch leaves the switch, not the loop.

The one idea

The array has 20 slots, the counter says how many are real. Every loop stops at the counter, and adding a vehicle means one slot filled and the counter up by one.
