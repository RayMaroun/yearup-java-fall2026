Workbook 2a · Section 2-2 · Exercise 2

Cell Phone Service, Dialing

Two phones from one blueprint, one display method for both, and each phone calling the other.

What you are building

Your exercise 1 program in a new project, grown three ways: CellPhone learns to dial a number, main builds a second phone, and a static display method prints whichever phone you hand it. Then phone 1 dials phone 2 and phone 2 dials phone 1.

The workbook says open the previous project. Here every exercise gets its own project and repository, so exercise 1 stays as you sent it. The lesson: one class, many objects, each with its own values.

What a finished run looks like

run
(the five questions, for each phone) Owner: Sandra Model: iPhone 15 Pro Max Number: 888-555-1234 Owner: Dana Wyatt Model: iPhone X Number: 800-555-5555 Sandra's phone is calling 800-555-5555 Dana Wyatt's phone is calling 888-555-1234

Your layout and values will differ. Check that each call shows the other phone's number.

Where people get stuck

  • Both phones show the same values. There is only one object: the second variable has no new of its own, or the setters went to phone 1 twice.
  • "non-static method cannot be referenced from a static context". display needs static to be called from main.
  • A phone calls its own number. dial was given the caller's number instead of the other phone's.
  • Red everywhere after adding display. It landed inside main. It goes after main's closing brace, still inside the class.
