Neighborhood Library

A library on the honor system: list the books, check one out with your name, check it back in by its id.

What you are building

A menu program for a free neighborhood library. A Book class holds one book; an array of at least 20 books holds the shelf. From the home screen the user sees the available books and checks one out with their name, or the checked out books and checks one in by id.

No logins and no due dates: anyone can check out a book by typing a name. Books live in the code, so every run starts with a full shelf.

Where people get stuck

  • A checked out book still shows as available. The list must skip books whose isCheckedOut is true.
  • The setter is setCheckedOut. For a boolean named isCheckedOut, IntelliJ names the getter isCheckedOut() and drops the "is" in the setter. That is normal.
  • The name prompt is skipped. The leftover Enter after nextInt().
  • c does nothing. Compare the letter ignoring capitals.

The one idea

Each book knows its own state. Checking out and checking in only flip two fields on one Book; the screens just choose which books to show.
