Roll the Dice

Roll a pair of dice 100 times, print every roll, and count how often the sum is 2, 4, 6 or 7.

What you are building

A program that rolls two dice 100 times and prints each roll with its sum. A small class Dice does the rolling with its method roll(). At the end, it prints four counters: how many times the sum was 2, 4, 6 and 7.

It asks the user nothing. Every run gives different numbers: that is the random part working, not a bug.

What a finished run looks like

run
Roll 1: 4 - 6 Sum: 10 Roll 2: 1 - 1 Sum: 2 Roll 3: 3 - 4 Sum: 7 ... Roll 100: 5 - 2 Sum: 7 Sum of 2: 3 Sum of 4: 9 Sum of 6: 13 Sum of 7: 18 Process finished with exit code 0

The rolls are random, so yours will differ on every run. The counter wording is yours too.

Where people get stuck

  • Every roll is 0 or 1. The cast to int happens too early. Copy the parentheses of the formula exactly.
  • A 7 on a die. Check minValue and maxValue: the range is 1 to 6.
  • Counters always 0 or 1. They are declared inside the loop, so they restart every roll.
  • Counters print 100 times. The print is inside the loop.

The one idea

The class does one job, rolling; main does the counting. Random results are checked by their pattern: over 100 rolls, 7 should come up far more often than 2.
