Each round is one loop with one blank and the output it should print. Pick the piece that fills the blank, by click or keys 1 to 3. The loop then runs with your piece.

break leaves the whole loop · continue skips the rest of this pass · % 3 == 0 true at 3, 6, 9 · == 3 true once · space or Enter goes on after a miss

The one idea

break ends the loop; continue ends only the current pass. After break, nothing else in the loop runs. After continue, the update runs and the loop checks its condition again, so the next pass still happens.
