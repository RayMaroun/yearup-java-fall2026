The rule IntelliJ follows: a getter for every type, except a boolean, which gets an ister. The setter is always set. You never type these names; you only name the field.

Click a field on the left. The names come from the field name and its type, nothing else.

The one idea

A boolean field gets an is getter, every other field gets a get getter, and the setter is always set. Name the field without is (learner, not isLearner) and let IntelliJ generate the rest.
