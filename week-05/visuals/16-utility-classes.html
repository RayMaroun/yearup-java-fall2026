Two things make a utility class: a private constructor and only static members. Press a button to see why.

The one idea

A utility class is never instantiated: its constructor is private, and everything in it is static, so you call it on the class name. Math.round(12.8) works, new Math() does not compile. Your NameFormatter is built the same way.
