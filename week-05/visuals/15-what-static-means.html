What Static Means

Some things belong to each player. Some belong to the team. Add players and look at both.

Team

Falcons team name
The team exists before anyone joins. A jersey number cannot: nobody is wearing one yet. Add a player.

The one idea

A jersey number belongs to one player: every player has their own, and with no players there are none. The team name belongs to the team: there is exactly one, it exists before any player joins, and every player shares it. In Java, a thing that belongs to the whole class instead of to each object is called static. One copy, shared, there from the start.
