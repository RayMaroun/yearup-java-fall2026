Right now price is an instance member: it only exists inside an object. With no object, there is no price. Press new Ticket().

The one idea

static means the member belongs to the class, not to an object. It exists once, from the moment the program starts, whether you build zero objects or a hundred, and you reach it through the class name: Ticket.price, not ticket1.price.
