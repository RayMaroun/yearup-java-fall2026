The one idea

Has-a means one class keeps another inside it: a Hand has Cards, a Deck has Cards. That is what lets them talk: the Hand can ask each of its Cards for its points. The Card knows nothing about the Hand, and the Deck and the Hand never talk to each other. A card only moves because your main takes it from the deck and hands it to the hand.
