Every attribute here has a built-in type: number, text, yes or no. That is all you have used so far. Press a Deck has cards.

The one idea

A has-a relationship is an attribute whose type is another class. A Deck has cards, so Deck holds Card and depends on it. A Hand has cards, the same way. Card gains nothing and knows nothing: the dependency runs one way, from the holder to the held.
