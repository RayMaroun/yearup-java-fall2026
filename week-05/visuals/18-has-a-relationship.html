The one idea

Has-a means one object holds another. The holder can use what it holds, and cannot do its job without it. The held object does not know who holds it. A Hand has Cards, so a Hand depends on Card. A Card depends on nothing. A Deck holds Cards too, and Deck and Hand never meet.
