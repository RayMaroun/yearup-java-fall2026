Has-A

Objects can be made of other objects. Put the parts in, take them out, click a part.

on their own · each one is a complete object

a Car

its own facts: color blue, year 2024

a Car has: nothing yet

An Engine and four Wheels, each a complete object with its own facts. The Car is also an object, with facts of its own. Right now none of them has anything to do with the others.

The one idea

Has-a: an object keeps other objects inside it, as its parts. A Car has an Engine and has Wheels. The parts stay what they are, each with its own facts; the Car is the thing that is made of them.
