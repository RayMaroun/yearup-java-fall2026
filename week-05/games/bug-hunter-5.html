The one idea

Week 5 bugs hide in who owns what. A field is private to its class and set with this., a method that needs an object is called on an object, a BigDecimal is built from a String, rounded with a RoundingMode, reassigned after every operation and compared with compareTo. A test is public or package-private, lives in src/test/java, and names the expected value first.
