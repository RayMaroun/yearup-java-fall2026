UNIT = the methods of one object · INTEGRATION = objects or sub-systems working together · END-TO-END = the full experience of a user

The one idea

Ask how much of the system the test touches. One object's methods is a Unit test: fast, and most of your tests. Objects or sub-systems talking to each other, such as a real database or file, is an Integration test: slower, fewer. A user's whole path through the app is an End-to-End test: the most setup, the fewest tests, at the top.
