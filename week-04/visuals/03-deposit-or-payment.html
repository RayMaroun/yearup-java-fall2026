Three lines in the file. The deposit is positive, the two payments are negative. The switch is on Deposit.

The one idea

The user always types a positive number. A deposit is stored as it is, a payment is stored with a minus in front. That sign is the only thing that separates deposits from payments later, so the Transaction has no extra field for the type.
