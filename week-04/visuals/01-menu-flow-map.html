The one idea

Three screens, three loops, one inside the other. Each screen is a loop that prints its menu, reads one choice, and runs a switch where every letter is a case. Most cases do their job and the loop prints the menu again. Only X leaves the Home loop, only H leaves the Ledger loop, only 0 leaves the Reports loop.
