One line, two parts: a verb first, then what changed. Capital letter, under 50 characters, no period. Someone reading the history should know what changed without opening the code.

The one idea

A commit message is one line a stranger reads in the history and knows what changed. Verb first, the thing second: Add loadTransactions to read the csv, Fix payment stored as positive, Show ledger newest first. "update", "final" and "asdf" are the lines Raymond will ask about. One commit per task, at least four a day.
